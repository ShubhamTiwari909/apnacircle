# Privacy Notice

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Version [VERSION] · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This notice explains how [LEGAL ENTITY] ("ApnaCircle", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you use our website and services. We are the Data Fiduciary for this data. If anything here is unclear, write to [PRIVACY EMAIL].

## 1. The short version

- You must be 18 or older.
- We collect only what we need to run ApnaCircle: your account, a profile you choose to build, meetup activity, and safety records.
- **We do not collect your phone number.**
- Your profile is **not visible to other members unless you turn on discovery**. It is never visible to people who are not signed in.
- Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other members.
- We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.
- You can see, correct, export and delete your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

## 3. Consent and how to withdraw it

We ask for your consent separately for: showing your profile in discovery, showing your city, non-essential email, and analytics. Each is optional and off by default. You can change any of them in **Settings** at any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it; it does not affect what we did before you withdrew. Some data is processed because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for or to meet legal obligations, and withdrawal of an optional consent will not stop that.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Signed-out visitors** can see public pages only: city pages, places, guides and public meetup details (title, time, public venue and area, host display name, attendee count). They cannot see member profiles, photos or bios.
- **Signed-in members** can see profiles and photos only according to your discovery setting and the visibility you set for each photo. Blocked members cannot see you and you cannot see them.
- **Meetup hosts and approved attendees** can see each other's display names, and the exact meeting spot, for that meetup.
- **Contact handles** (Instagram or WhatsApp) are shown only to the single connection you shared them with. Once someone has seen a handle we cannot technically take it back from them, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.

## 7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **Access** a copy of your data (Settings, Download my data).
- **Correct** or update your profile and account details.
- **Erase** your account and data (Settings, Delete account).
- **Withdraw consent** for optional uses.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
- **Complain** to us (see section 12). If we do not resolve your complaint, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

We aim to respond to rights requests within 7 days and will tell you if we need more time. [Owner/counsel: confirm this commitment.]

## 8. Children

ApnaCircle is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and block under-18 sign-ups. If you believe a minor is using the service, report it from any profile or from the Safety Center and we will treat it as an urgent case. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children, and we do not track or target advertising at anyone.

## 9. Retention and deletion

- When you delete your account we keep it for **14 days** in case you change your mind, then permanently delete your profile, photos, preferences, push subscriptions and analytics identifiers from our systems.
- Reports, evidence and moderation records are kept for up to **12 months** after a case closes, with minimal identifiers, and longer only if the law requires or a legal hold applies.
- Security and audit logs are kept for up to **12 months**; application logs for up to **90 days**.
- Backups roll off within about **35 days**. If we ever restore a backup, we re-apply deletions so deleted accounts do not come back.
- We may keep a hashed identifier to stop a banned account from re-registering, and hashes of banned photos to stop re-uploads.

## 10. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, multi-factor authentication for staff, private photo storage with short-lived access links, rate limiting, logging and regular tested backups. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
