## 2. What you can complain about

- Content or conduct that breaks our rules, is unlawful, or harms you.
- Intimate images of you shared without consent, or content that impersonates you.
- Intellectual-property or other legal complaints about content.
- A decision we made about your account, content or meetup (appeal).
- A privacy or data-rights matter (see also [Privacy Notice](/privacy)).

## 3. How to complain

Use the **Report** button where the content appears, or email [GRIEVANCE EMAIL]. Please include a link or description of the content, what is wrong, and how we can contact you. For urgent safety matters, call **112** first.

## 4. What happens next and when

| Step | Our commitment |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within **24 hours** of receiving your complaint |
| Intimate images without consent, or impersonation | We remove or disable access within **24 hours** of a complaint |
| Urgent safety reports (suspected minors, threats, imminent harm) | Acknowledged within **2 hours** during support hours; action within 24 hours |
| Other reports | Reviewed within the targets in our moderation process, usually 24 to 72 hours |
| Resolution of your grievance | Within **15 days** of receipt |
| Privacy and data-rights requests | Target response within 7 days |

[Owner and counsel: confirm that your team can meet each commitment, including support hours, before publishing.]

We will tell you the outcome and, where appropriate, the reason. We keep a record of grievances and our actions.

## 5. Appeals

If we removed your content, restricted or suspended your account, or cancelled your meetup, you can appeal within **14 days** by replying to the notice or emailing [GRIEVANCE EMAIL] with your reasons and any evidence. A different team member reviews appeals where possible. We will tell you the outcome. Some decisions cannot be reversed, such as removal of unlawful content or bans for zero-tolerance violations after review.
