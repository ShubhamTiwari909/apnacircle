# Safety Center

Your safety matters more than any connection or meetup. Most people on ApnaCircle are friendly, but you should always take sensible precautions, especially when meeting someone new.

## Before you meet

- **Start with group meetups.** They are public, hosted and easier to leave.
- **Check the host and the event.** Read the description, look at the attendee count, and be wary of events that change venue or details at the last minute.
- **Protect your details.** Do not share your home address, workplace, daily routine, financial details or documents. Share an Instagram or WhatsApp handle only when you are comfortable, and only with that one person.
- **Be careful with photos.** Choose per-photo visibility you are happy with. Avoid photos showing your home, school, workplace or vehicle number.
- **Trust your instincts.** If someone pressures you, asks for money, rushes you or makes you uneasy, stop and report.

## When you meet

- Meet in **public, well-lit places** with other people around.
- **Tell someone you trust** where you are going, who you are meeting and when you expect to be back.
- Arrange your own travel; do not rely on a stranger for a ride. Keep your phone charged.
- Stay sober enough to look after yourself. Do not leave drinks unattended.
- Leave if you feel uncomfortable. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

## Spotting scams and pressure

Be cautious if someone: asks for money or gifts, pushes an investment, job or loan, sends links or asks you to move to another app straight away, claims an emergency, refuses to meet publicly, or uses photos that look too perfect. Never send money to someone you have not met in person and trust.

## If something goes wrong

1. **Immediate danger:** call **112** (police, fire, ambulance).
2. **Report to us:** use **Report** on the profile, photo or meetup, or [Grievance and Appeals](/grievance). Reports about minors, intimate images, threats or harassment are treated as urgent.
3. **Block:** blocking is instant, private and mutual. Use it whenever you want to.
4. **Cybercrime:** you can also report online fraud, harassment or image misuse at **cybercrime.gov.in** or by calling the National Cyber Crime Helpline on **1930**.
5. **Keep evidence:** save screenshots and details of what happened. We also preserve a snapshot of what was reported.
6. **Seek support:** talk to someone you trust. If you are a woman and need help, you can also contact the women's helpline (**181**).

## Hosting safely

Read the [Host Guidelines](/host-guidelines). Choose public venues, keep groups small while you are new, know your exits and emergency numbers, and report any incident the same day.

## What we do

We review reports, act quickly on urgent ones, restrict or ban people who break the rules, hold photos from new accounts for review, and limit how many requests new members can send. We cannot verify everyone or control what happens offline, so please stay alert and look after one another.
