# Host Guidelines and Agreement

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Version [VERSION] · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Liability and indemnity wording needs counsel's drafting.

Hosts make ApnaCircle work. By applying to host, and again before your first event, you accept this agreement together with our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## 1. Becoming a host

- You must be 18 or older with a complete profile and an approved photo.
- We review every new host (for example a short call, a check of your public social profile, and a local reference where possible). We may decline or end hosting at any time.
- Your **first three events** are limited to 15 attendees, must be in a public venue, and are reviewed by us before they are published.

## 2. What you may host

Allowed: cafe and food outings, photography walks, city walking or cycling groups, sports and fitness in public grounds, movies and cultural outings, local exploration inside the city, and tech, professional or hobby meetups. Women-only and women-led meetups are available with extra review.

Not allowed in V1: trekking, overnight or outstation trips, organised or arranged transport, events at private homes, events centred on alcohol, events open to under-18s, and events that charge fees through or because of ApnaCircle.

## 3. Your responsibilities

- **Public venue only.** Give the venue name and locality publicly. Share the exact meeting spot only through the attendee-only note. Never publish a home address.
- **Be accurate.** Describe the activity, time, cost (if attendees pay their own way), difficulty and any age or fitness expectations honestly. Do not promise things you cannot deliver.
- **Keep it safe.** Check that the venue is open and suitable, plan for weather and crowding, have a plan for someone who feels unwell or uncomfortable, and know local emergency contacts (**112**). If an activity carries physical risk (such as cycling or sports), tell attendees what protective equipment or fitness is expected.
- **Manage attendance.** Respect the capacity you set. Approve attendees fairly. You may remove anyone who breaks the rules or makes others uncomfortable.
- **Mark attendance.** Check attendees in after the event so records are accurate.
- **Cancel early.** If you must cancel or change an event, do it in the app with a reason as soon as you know. Last-minute cancellations are flagged to attendees as priority.
- **Follow the law and venue rules.** You are responsible for any permissions, licences or permits your activity needs.
- **Protect members.** Never collect attendees' personal data for other purposes, never solicit money or products, never pressure anyone to share contact details, and keep attendee information confidential.
- **Report.** Report any incident, harassment or safety concern promptly through the app. For anything serious or urgent, call 112 first.

## 4. Incidents

If something goes wrong at your event, put people's safety first, get help, and then report it to us the same day. We may ask for information to investigate. We may pause your events or hosting while we review.

## 5. Independent role

You host as an independent individual. You are not our employee, agent or partner. We do not organise your event, supervise it or insure it, and we do not control the venue or attendees.
