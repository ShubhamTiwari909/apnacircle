# Law Enforcement and Legal Requests Policy

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Version [VERSION] · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. This page is public; the internal runbook that supports it should be written with counsel.

We respect our members' privacy and we also cooperate with lawful requests that protect people. This page explains how we handle them.

## 1. Who can ask

Courts, government agencies and police authorities in India, acting within their legal powers. We do not respond to informal requests from private individuals, other companies or foreign authorities except through valid Indian legal processes (for example mutual legal assistance).

## 2. What we require

A written request or order that identifies the authority, the legal basis and the specific account or content, signed by a competent officer, sent to [LEGAL REQUESTS EMAIL]. We verify the sender and the authority before acting. We may ask for clarification or narrow the request.

## 3. What we do

- A designated person, with counsel's advice where needed, reviews each request.
- We provide only the data the request legally requires and that we hold. Our systems keep minimal data: we do not hold phone numbers, message content (there is no chat) or precise locations.
- We act on valid takedown orders within the legally required timelines. [Counsel to confirm timelines, including for government and court orders.]
- We keep a record of each request, our decision and what we disclosed.
- We preserve relevant data for the legally required period on a valid preservation request.

## 4. Telling members

Where the law allows, we tell the affected member before disclosing their data so they can seek legal advice. We do not do so where the law or the order forbids it, or where notice would create a risk to someone's safety or obstruct an investigation of serious harm.

## 5. Emergencies

If we believe there is a serious, imminent risk to someone's life or physical safety, we may share limited information with the police to help prevent harm. We record these disclosures and review them afterwards.

## 6. Transparency

We may publish summary statistics about requests received and actions taken.
