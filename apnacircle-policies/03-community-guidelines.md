# Community Guidelines

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Version [VERSION] · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

ApnaCircle exists to help people in our city make real friendships and enjoy real activities. These guidelines apply to everything you post or do on ApnaCircle and at meetups arranged through it.

## Our principles

1. **Respect.** Treat everyone with dignity, whatever their gender, religion, caste, community, language, appearance, disability or background.
2. **Consent.** "No" means no. Never pressure anyone to connect, share details or attend anything.
3. **Honesty.** Be who you say you are. Use real photos of yourself.
4. **Safety first.** Meet in public places and look out for each other.

## Not allowed

- **Harassment and abuse:** insults, threats, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, sexual comments, intimidation, doxxing.
- **Hate:** content that attacks or demeans people for who they are, or promotes violence or discrimination.
- **Sexual content:** nudity, sexually explicit photos or messages, or sexual solicitation. Never share intimate images of anyone without their consent.
- **Impersonation and deception:** fake identities, someone else's photos, fake events or reviews, catfishing.
- **Minors:** anyone under 18 using the service, or photos of minors in profiles or events.
- **Scams and solicitation:** requests for money, investment or loan pitches, multi-level marketing, gambling, fake jobs, selling or advertising products and services.
- **Illegal activity:** anything unlawful, including drugs, weapons, and unlawful gatherings.
- **Privacy violations:** sharing another person's private details, photos or contact information, or recording people without permission.
- **Platform abuse:** spam, mass requests, scraping, fake accounts, evading blocks or bans, bypassing limits or security.
- **Dangerous events:** meetups at private homes, overnight, outstation or trekking trips, organised transport, or events centred on alcohol.

## Connection requests

Send requests only to people you genuinely want to know. Keep your introduction short and respectful. Accept, ignore or decline freely: nobody owes you a reply, and repeated requests after a decline are not allowed.

## At meetups

Be on time, respect the host and other attendees, follow venue rules and local law, and do not pressure anyone to stay, drink, leave with you or share contact details. If you feel unsafe, leave and report it.

## Reporting

Use **Report** on any profile, photo or meetup, or the Safety Center. Reports are reviewed by our team; the person reported is not told who reported them. Please do not make false or retaliatory reports.

## What happens when rules are broken

Depending on severity we may remove content, warn you, restrict features for a period, suspend your account or permanently ban you. Serious cases (suspected minors, intimate imagery without consent, threats, targeted harassment, impersonation) may lead to immediate suspension before review. We may report serious or unlawful conduct to the authorities. You can appeal within 14 days (see [Grievance and Appeals](/grievance)).

## Emergencies

If someone is in immediate danger, call **112**. ApnaCircle is not an emergency service.
