Starting a local community in a tier-2 or tier-3 city works best when meetups feel ordinary, safe, and easy to leave whenever you want.

## Pick clear public landmarks

Choose well-lit, busy public parks or sit-down cafes with straightforward entry and exit. Always share the general venue publicly and keep the exact meeting-spot note (such as a specific gate) visible only to approved attendees.

## Keep first groups small

In early stages, groups of 6 to 15 people allow everyone to introduce themselves without feeling overwhelmed. Hosts confirm attendance after the meetup so trust builds organically.

## Safety basics

- Tell a friend or family member where you are going and when you expect to be back.
- Prefer daylight hours for parks and walking routes.
- Use the in-app report or block controls whenever something feels off, and dial **112** for urgent emergencies in India.
