# Host handbook (draft for counsel and owner review)

## Before you create a meetup
- Pick a **public** venue (park, cafe, library lawn, market, sports ground). Never a home.
- Choose a date at least 6 hours ahead, finishing the same day and within 8 hours.
- Write what people will actually do, how long it takes, and who it suits.
- Choose **auto-approve** (people are confirmed instantly while there is room) or **approval** (you review each request).
- Put the exact meeting spot, such as "Gate 2, near the ticket counter", in the meeting-spot note. Only approved attendees and you can see it.

## Rules that protect everyone
- Adults only (18+). No trekking, overnight, outstation or transport-included events. No events centred on alcohol.
- Women-only meetups: use the women-only option, keep it small (12 maximum), and expect an admin to approve it. Eligibility is self-declared and cannot be fully verified, so the safeguards are host vetting and size, not the label.
- Never ask attendees for money, ID, home addresses or phone numbers through ApnaCircle.

## If you must cancel
Use **Cancel** and give a clear reason. Every attendee is told in-app, by email and by push straight away. Cancel as early as you can.

## On the day
1. Arrive early. Greet people at the meeting spot.
2. Check people in (open from 30 minutes before the start): only checked-in people count as attended.
3. If anyone is unsafe or unwelcome, remove them from the meetup and report them.
4. After the event, press **Complete**. People who did not show are marked as no-shows and attendees get a private "did you feel safe?" prompt. Feedback goes to the safety lead, not to you.

## Emergencies
Call 112. Report concerns through the Safety Center and the grievance page.
