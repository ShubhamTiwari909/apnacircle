# Meetup day checklist

- [ ] Venue open and suitable; a backup nearby if weather turns.
- [ ] Meeting-spot note is accurate (edit the meetup if it changed; attendees are notified).
- [ ] Attendee list reviewed; you know how many to expect.
- [ ] You know the nearest hospital and have 112 ready.
- [ ] Check-in opened 30 minutes before the start.
- [ ] Anyone who feels unsafe can leave freely; you know how to remove an attendee and report.
- [ ] After the event: press Complete, note anything the safety lead should know.
